## DR1 Execution Results (RUN_0_P1F_EXT1)

- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Candidate:** Variant L2
- **Steps:** 1000
- **Outcome:** **NONE_VIABLE**
- **V1-V4:** PASS (V4 passed with 49/54 labels having logit std > 0.01)
- **V5:** FAIL. Model scored exactly 63/200 Top-1 hits, which strictly equals the label-frequency prior (63/200). Strict `>` is required.

**Key Findings:**
1. **Data Anomaly:** `cmp-lg` and `cond-mat.dis-nn` are completely identical columns in `train.csv` (17,152 positives each) and `dev.csv` (346 positives each). The same applies to `math.co` and `math.it`.
2. **Architectural Limitation:** The identical ranking across 200 documents (Top-1 is `cond-mat.dis-nn` for 200/200 docs) is heavily influenced by the architecture. In `CSAM_L.forward`, `logits = W(alpha_i * h_Li + A @ h_L)`. The term `A @ h_L` is constant across all documents. Even with logit variance (49/54 passing V4), the baseline gap between labels created by the static term is massive relative to the small document-specific `alpha` term for this checkpoint. While document-insensitive ranking was observed in DR1, universal mathematical impossibility has not been strictly proven.
